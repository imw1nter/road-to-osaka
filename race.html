Course overview

Starts in front of the Osaka Prefectural Government Building and finishes inside Osaka Castle Park. Certified by JAAF, World Athletics and AIMS.

Pins show landmarks on or near the course (approximate). This is not the exact route – see the official website for the course map.

The course, section by section

  1. Start · 0 kmOsaka Prefectural Government Building, next to Osaka Castle Park. Crowded start – don't waste energy weaving.
  2. Okawa River → Nakanoshima · ~6 kmAlong the river into Nakanoshima, where the 7.2 km “720” race finishes.
  3. Midosuji · ~7–12 kmOsaka's wide main boulevard – flat and fast. Settle into goal pace.
  4. Namba & DotonboriThe route doubles back here, past the famous Glico sign. Big crowds – stay calm.
  5. Southern neighbourhoods · ~15–27 kmQuieter streets heading south, past the Kyocera Dome area. Keep fuelling.
  6. Shitennoji & ImazatosujiPast Shitennoji Temple, then a long, straight stretch of about 5 km on Imazatosuji.
  7. After 30 kmThe one real climb of the course comes just after 30 km. Ease off and keep your effort steady.
  8. Osaka Business Park → Finish · 42.195 kmFinal kilometres to the finish inside Osaka Castle Park.
